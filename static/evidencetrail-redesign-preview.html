The two checks

Verify the quotation. Then inspect the claim.

01 · SOURCE CHECKDoes this wording appear on the cited page?
02 · REQUIREMENT CHECKDoes the source attribute the work to the applicant?
A verified quotation proves its location in the supplied file. It does not prove real-world truth.
