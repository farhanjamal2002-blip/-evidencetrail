# EvidenceTrail — Muse Spark Hackathon Submission Guide

EvidenceTrail is built around Muse Spark requirement mapping and source-verified CV evidence. This guide maps the public event criteria and the event-detail screenshots shared by the entrant to the product design. The organizer’s official rules and waiver remain the controlling documents; the selected entrant must read and accept them in the submission flow.

## Rules mapped to the project

| Area | EvidenceTrail implementation | Submission check |
|---|---|---|
| Theme fit | EvidenceTrail helps recruiters review a job description against a batch of up to five messy CVs. Muse identifies direct and potentially transferable experience; EvidenceTrail keeps gaps, conflicts, source passages, and human review in the workflow. It does not score or automatically rank candidates. | Show a recruiter completing a real requirement-by-requirement review, including one related finding, one gap or conflict, and a next question. |
| Muse Spark integration · 25% judging weight | Muse Spark 1.3 makes two structured requests per CV through Meta Model API. Pass one interprets the JD and proposes evidence. Pass two classifies evidence as direct, related, unsupported, or conflicting, labels evidence strength, and explains what a transfer does not prove. EvidenceTrail retrieves bounded source spans, rejects unknown source IDs and quote drift, verifies the cited PDF page, and derives visible statuses only from locally accepted citations. Keyword-only mentions cannot support a finding. | Show the live Muse output, its distinction between direct and transferable experience, the local citation checks, strength chips, and the uncertainty path. The second pass receives only locally selected, provenance-bearing spans. Keep the API key server-side. |
| Semantic calibration | `calibration/semantic_cases.json` contains five fictional cases across public-sector procurement, government contracting, semiconductor operations, and public healthcare procurement, including an image-only two-column scan. The latest five-case run made 10 requests with retries disabled: 16/18 expected categories matched and 23/24 proposed quotes passed local checks. Two expected date-conflict labels were marked Direct but gated to human review as ambiguous. A separate one-time scanned-CV rerun matched 3/3 expected labels; local checks verified 3/5 proposed quotes and blocked 2/5 because they were not found on cited page 1. | Use the written reports and JSON files linked in `calibration/REPORT_INDEX.md`. Recruiters can mark advance, hold, or do not advance, add a note, and export those human decisions with per-requirement citations and verification results as JSON. It creates no automated candidate score or ranking. | Upload a small batch, inspect separate evidence trails, record a recruiter decision, and download the shortlist audit plus individual HTML evidence packs. |

| Role-first workflow | Before CV upload, a free deterministic preflight sends only job text to the EvidenceTrail app server. It maps numbered/bulleted lines into reviewer evidence prompts, draft interview questions, candidate self-checks, exact duplicate flags, and labels based only on explicit priority wording in a criterion or its section heading. It can export a standalone job-only worksheet. No Meta request or persistent review storage is involved. | Show one criterion, the explicit wording behind its priority label, and the downloaded worksheet. State that the question is a draft, unlisted paragraph requirements may be missed, and labels do not infer importance. |

Ambiguous findings stay visible for human review. | Open a direct passage and a related passage in the source viewer; show a gap, conflict, and neutral follow-up question. ## Product claims to preserve

- EvidenceTrail checks whether a quoted passage occurs in the supplied document; it does not verify a real-world credential or employment claim.
- “Not found” means the supplied CV did not provide verified support; it does not mean the candidate lacks the qualification.
- The batch view lists CVs in upload order and stores recruiter-entered shortlist choices; Muse and EvidenceTrail do not score or rank candidates or decide who is hired.
