# Unified demo job rubric — demo-v2

**Role:** OSAT Supplier and Operations Manager
**Organization:** GlobalFoundries
**Source:** [Official job posting](https://globalfoundries.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/External/job/OSAT-Supplier-and-Operations-Manager_JR-2605085)

One public-job-derived role, two fixed criteria, and five fictional CVs. The same text is used for the live Muse run and the frozen replay.

## Requirements

1. **Must-have:** Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in semiconductor operations, outsourced manufacturing, OSAT management, sourcing or commodity management, supplier relationship management, contract negotiation, or continuous improvement.
2. **Must-have:** Support supplier negotiations, pricing analysis, and commercial contract implementation.

## Review labels

- **Direct:** the CV gives a work example that directly supports the requirement.
- **Related:** the CV describes adjacent work that could transfer; name what remains unproven.
- **Unsupported:** no usable evidence was found. A keyword list or generic claim alone is not a work example.
- **Conflict to clarify:** the CV contains inconsistent information relevant to the criterion. It is a request for human review, not a conclusion about intent.
- **Needs a closer look:** the source or interpretation is too uncertain for a stronger label.

Evidence strength is separate: **Specific** (concrete work, scope, or result), **Stated** (a claim without a concrete example), **Keyword only** (a skill phrase without work evidence), or **None** (no passage). A source check confirms text and page location only; it does not authenticate the underlying claim.

## Human-review rules

- Review all five candidates in upload order; this rubric does not rank candidates.
- A date-overlap flag routes the affected requirement to review; it never rejects a candidate automatically.
- A rejected citation stays out of displayed evidence and highlights.
- Keep transfer explanations and unsupported gaps visible. Muse proposes; the recruiter decides.
