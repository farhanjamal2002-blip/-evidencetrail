# Unified demo job rubric — demo-v1

**Role:** OSAT Supplier and Operations Manager
**Organization:** GlobalFoundries
**Source:** [Official job posting](https://globalfoundries.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/External/job/OSAT-Supplier-and-Operations-Manager_JR-2605085)

This frozen demo rubric uses two requirements from the same public role. Requirement 2 reuses the focal wording from the earlier Strength Ladder demo.

## Requirements

1. **Must-have:** Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in semiconductor operations, outsourced manufacturing, OSAT management, sourcing or commodity management, supplier relationship management, contract negotiation, or continuous improvement.
2. **Must-have:** Support supplier negotiations, pricing analysis, and commercial contract implementation.

## Review instructions

- Use the same two requirements for all five fictional CVs.
- Record evidence type separately from evidence strength.
- Evidence types: Direct, Related, Unsupported, Conflict to clarify, Needs review.
- Evidence strength: Specific, Stated, Keyword only, or None.
- A matching phrase alone is not a work example. Keep keyword-only evidence from supporting a claim.
- Related means there is a plausible skill transfer; state what is still unproven.
- A timeline flag calls for clarification. It does not establish dishonesty or trigger rejection.
- Make no candidate ranking from the rubric.
