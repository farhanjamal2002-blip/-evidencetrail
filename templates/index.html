      AT A GLANCE

      DATE CHECK

      Timeline review

      Dates are checked with local arithmetic against CV quotes. Month totals are approximate when the CV gives years only. An overlap or difference is a prompt to clarify, not a finding of dishonesty; a CV may omit earlier roles.

          APPLICATION CLAIM CHECK

          Before you send this

          This checks whether the supplied CV supports what the draft says. It cannot verify real-world truth; “not found” does not mean false.

          About these results
          Direct evidenceThe CV describes the job need in a sufficiently similar context. Check the original passage.
          Related experienceMuse sees a possible transfer. Review the explanation and what remains unproven.
          Not mentionedThis CV doesn't show it. The person may still have the experience.
          Details differTwo parts of the CV say different things. Check both passages.
          Needs a closer lookThe wording or source is unclear, so a person should review it.
