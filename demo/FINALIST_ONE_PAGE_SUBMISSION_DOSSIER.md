## Project title

**EvidenceTrail: The Evidence Verifier for AI-Assisted Hiring**

## Proposed 120-word description

EvidenceTrail helps recruiters understand what a candidate's **submitted documents actually establish**, without reducing people to an AI fit score. Meta Muse Spark 1.3 interprets an employer-approved job checklist and proposes requirement-level quotations from CVs. EvidenceTrail then independently checks the cited PDF pages, preserves original model findings, applies deterministic role-scope and contradiction safeguards, and shows the exact source to a human reviewer. When a claim is absent or conflicting, the recruiter can draft a targeted clarification instead of automatically rejecting someone. A received reply is recorded as self-attested, not magically verified. The result is an inspectable audit trail and a standalone evidence pack. Our working judge demo contains twenty explicitly fictional CVs and authentic **recorded** Meta responses; viewing it makes no new provider calls.

## Theme and technical differentiation

- **Proposed theme:** Build for Your World. Do not assert an accepted track without the event portal.
- **Core problem:** AI may quote genuine document text yet misinterpret who performed the work, the number of years, or whether two passages conflict.
- **Meta's role:** Muse Spark 1.3 interprets job criteria and CV context and returns candidate requirement findings and page-labelled citations. Results shown in the public judge inbox are **genuine saved Meta recordings**, not live inference.
- **EvidenceTrail's role:** Separate local PDF/OCR source validation, source highlighting, preservation of raw/final model status, timeline and role-scope checks, and human-controlled review/clarification.
- **Differentiator:** Recruiters can inspect a specific **real original-model versus corrected finding** (ET-003). This is not a new ATS matching score.
- **What it does NOT do:** Rank, score, recommend hiring, independently verify real-world employment claims, automatically reject, send messages, or maintain a complete production ATS.
- **Native Muse vision:** tested with one fictional image-only CV; image input succeeded but a quote failed the local grounding check and quality regressed relative to OCR-only. Vision is correctly **disabled on the public judge host**. Do not claim it improved accuracy.
